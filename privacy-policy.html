TSE-X Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 13, 2026

TSE-X ("we", "our", or "us") is a decentralized physical infrastructure network (DePIN) platform that enables crypto-powered payments for IoT devices. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the TSE-X mobile application.

Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address, display name, and profile photo (if provided). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email from your Google account.

Wallet Information: When you connect a crypto wallet (Phantom or MetaMask), we store your public wallet address. We never have access to your private keys, seed phrases, or wallet passwords.

Device Interaction Data: When you interact with IoT devices through the app (via BLE, NFC, or QR code), we collect device identifiers, session data, and transaction records to facilitate payments and device access.

Transaction Data: We record payment transactions including wallet addresses, amounts, token types, and timestamps. All blockchain transactions are publicly visible on their respective networks (Solana, Base).

Device Permissions: The app may request access to Bluetooth, Camera (for QR scanning), NFC, and Location (required for Bluetooth scanning on Android). These are used solely for device discovery and interaction.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

• Provide and maintain the TSE-X platform and services
• Process crypto payments between your wallet and IoT devices
• Manage your account and display your profile
• Operate the weekly prize draw feature
• Send push notifications about draw results and app updates (with your consent)
• Detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized access
• Improve the app experience

Weekly Prize Draw

If you enter the TSE-X Weekly Draw, we collect your wallet address and user ID to manage entries. Winner information (display name and amount won) is published in our public winners ledger. Winner email addresses are stored privately and never shared publicly.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore and Authentication) with industry-standard security measures. Transaction data is processed through our backend hosted on Render. We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for all communications.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

• Google Firebase: Authentication, database, and cloud messaging
• Phantom Wallet: Solana wallet connection and transaction signing
• MetaMask: Base/Ethereum wallet connection
• Helius: Solana RPC and webhook services
• Alchemy: Base blockchain RPC services
• CoinGecko: Token price data

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy governing their use of your data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following cases:

• Public blockchain data (wallet addresses and transactions are inherently public)
• Prize draw winners (display name and amount only, published in the winners ledger)
• Legal requirements (if required by law or legal process)

Your Rights

You can:

• Access and update your profile information within the app
• Disconnect your wallet at any time
• Delete your account directly from the Profile page in the app
• Opt out of push notifications through your device settings

Children's Privacy

TSE-X is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the app or via email.
